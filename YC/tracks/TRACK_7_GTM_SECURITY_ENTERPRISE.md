# Track 7: Enterprise GTM, Security & Compliance

> **Ownership:** Founders & GTM Lead  
> **Key Focus:** Pricing Tiers, SSO / RBAC, SOC 2 Type II Certification, HIPAA BAA, Enterprise Sales Playbook.

---

## 1. Scope & Objectives

1. **Commercial Packaging:** Open Core funnel -> Team (.4k–.8k/yr) -> Enterprise (–+ ACV).
2. **Enterprise Security:** SOC 2 Type II compliance, SAML 2.0 / OIDC SSO, Role-Based Access Control (Admin, Compliance Officer, Developer, Auditor).
3. **Legal & Privacy Agreements:** Standardized Master Services Agreement (MSA), Data Processing Agreement (DPA), and HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA).
4. **Sales Playbook & POCs:** 14-day Enterprise Proof-of-Concept playbook demonstrating instant gap discovery on customer codebases.
